# Parkdex icon

Since September 27, 2026 the landing favicon and Apple touch icon use Kip, the Parkdex field app's launcher icon: `parkdex-badge-mockup/art-source/kip/app-icon.png` (1024x1024, SHA256 `ec031feab8584687c2193fafd975a1988c9161cb608eb1f1c23fb519e283836f`).

Exports use direct LANCZOS resizing with no retouching. The source's off-white rounded corners are flood-filled: transparent in `favicon.ico` (16, 24, 32, 48 and 64px), `favicon-32x32.png` and `favicon-64x64.png`, and filled with the icon's orange in the 180px `apple-touch-icon.png`, because iOS applies its own mask.

The earlier camas soft-gloss icon (`parkdex-approved-icon-camas-soft-gloss-v1`) remains only as `favicon.svg`, used by the unlinked `/mockups/` comparison pages.
