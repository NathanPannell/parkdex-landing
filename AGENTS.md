# Landing page checks

For a photo-led landing hero, verify the opening viewport at 320x568, 390x844, 768x1024, 1280x800 and 1440x900. The primary CTA, a recognizable part of the landscape, and a legible product device must appear before scrolling. Do not place the full signup form ahead of all imagery on mobile.

Keep real app screenshots distinguishable from the landscape photograph. Preserve matte hardware and visible chassis contrast at the smallest shipped size. Update the surface brief when the user changes the opening composition.

Review the product content inside each screenshot, not only its device silhouette. In feature previews, keep the controls that demonstrate the named feature unobscured and use captions that match the actual signed-in or signed-out state. A decorative device collage is not evidence of a readable product flow.

For transparent device overlays, measure the real alpha opening before setting CSS screen insets. Fill the opening behind the bezel, then inspect all four edges at the largest displayed size for visible gaps.

For text-triggered hover and click tooltips, anchor the open surface inside its owning section near the trigger. Inspect the open state at phone and desktop widths, including after scrolling, so the tooltip follows the content rather than the viewport.

For an animated word or number inside a headline, inspect its initial and final text beside the neighboring words at 320px and desktop widths. Keep underlines fitted to the visible glyphs; a reserved control width must not create an apparent extra margin.

When the footer is intended to be a compact desktop row, check it at full desktop width with every link visible. Put lengthy email and credit content in contained dialogs so opening it does not change the row layout, and verify the dialogs at phone width.
