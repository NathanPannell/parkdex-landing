# Landing page checks

For a photo-led landing hero, verify the opening viewport at 320x568, 390x844, 768x1024, 1280x800 and 1440x900. The primary CTA, a recognizable part of the landscape, and a legible product device must appear before scrolling. Do not place the full signup form ahead of all imagery on mobile.

Keep real app screenshots distinguishable from the landscape photograph. Preserve matte hardware and visible chassis contrast at the smallest shipped size. Update the surface brief when the user changes the opening composition.
