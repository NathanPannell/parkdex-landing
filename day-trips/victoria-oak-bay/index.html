Day trips

Victoria and Oak Bay shoreline

An easy city day that follows the water east from downtown. Start among the gardens at Beacon Hill, then work along the Oak Bay shore to finish on the sand at Cadboro Bay.

  1. 1

    commonStop 1

    Beacon Hill Park

    Wander the garden paths and find the stone bridge over Goodacre Lake. Peacocks roam the park, and the southern edge looks out over the Strait of Juan de Fuca, close to Mile Zero.

    See it on the map
  2. 2

    commonStop 2

    Willows Park

    An open Oak Bay beach with lawns, big trees, a playground and the Kiwanis Tea Room. A good spot for a break before the next stop.

    See it on the map
  3. 3

    commonStop 3

    Uplands Park

    A Garry oak meadow that fills with camas and wildflowers in spring. The rocky ground leads toward Cattle Point and the water.

    See it on the map
  4. 4

    commonStop 4

    Cadboro Gyro Park

    Finish on the sandy Cadboro Bay waterfront. The playground’s red octopus, blue salmon and Caddy the sea serpent are hard to miss.

    See it on the map
