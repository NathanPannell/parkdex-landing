A day out in Sooke

A longer day west of the city, mixing a quiet lake, a rugged coast, an easy harbour walk and the river pools. Bring a lunch and good shoes.

  1. 1

    commonStop 1

    Matheson Lake Regional Park

    Start at the small sandy beach on Matheson Lake. A forested loop circles the water near the Galloping Goose Trail.

    See it on the map
  2. 2

    uncommonStop 2

    East Sooke Regional Park

    The Coast Trail runs past rocky bays and pocket beaches, with views across the strait to the Olympic Mountains. It is rugged, so pick a short section if time is tight.

    See it on the map
  3. 3

    commonStop 3

    Whiffin Spit Park

    A level path along a narrow, hooked spit at the mouth of Sooke Harbour, with wide views from the point.

    See it on the map
  4. 4

    rareStop 4

    Sooke Potholes Regional Park

    End at the rock pools the Sooke River carved into the stone. This one is the rare badge on the list.

    See it on the map

Check park hours, parking and trail conditions before you go. Each badge unlocks once you’re inside that park’s boundary.
