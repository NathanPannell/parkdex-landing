A day out in Sooke

This is a fuller day west of the city, and it covers a lot of different ground: a quiet lake, a rugged coastline, an easy walk out to the mouth of a harbour, and river pools carved into the rock.

You will want a car, a packed lunch, water, and shoes you do not mind getting wet. The order below works from the city outward and finishes inland.

1Matheson Lake Regional Park

Start gently at Matheson Lake. A small sandy beach opens onto the water, and a forested loop circles the lake close to the Galloping Goose Trail.

The badge shows that sandy beach, so it is the spot for your check-in photo.

On to the next one: Head out to East Sooke Road for the coast.

2East Sooke Regional Park

East Sooke is the dramatic one. The Coast Trail follows the shoreline past rocky bays and pocket beaches, with views across the Strait of Juan de Fuca to the Olympic Mountains.

The full trail is a big day on its own and it is rugged underfoot, so on this trip pick a short out-and-back from one of the trailheads and save the rest for another visit.

On to the next one: Whiffin Spit sits just across the harbour mouth from East Sooke, but the road goes the long way round through Sooke, so allow time for the drive.

3Whiffin Spit Park

After East Sooke, Whiffin Spit is a nice change of pace. A level path runs along a narrow, hooked spit at the mouth of Sooke Harbour, with the water on both sides.

Walk out to the rounded point for wide views over the harbour and the sea, and keep an eye out for marine wildlife along the way.

On to the next one: From town, head north up Sooke River Road to finish at the river.

4Sooke Potholes Regional Park

The Sooke Potholes are the reason to save energy. Swirling water and glacial boulders carved the river’s rock pools, and they sit between forest and polished stone ledges.

This is the rare badge on the list. Take care on the rocks; they can be slippery, and the river changes with the season.

Four badges, one of them rare, and a lot of coastline. Not bad for a day.

Check park hours, parking and trail conditions before you go. Each badge unlocks once you’re inside that park’s boundary.
