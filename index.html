Go to a park, plant your flag, and hold it while your friends try to take it. Every hour it flies, it earns you a point.

Free Android beta in Canada.

How a game works

Every park on the map is up for grabs. You only have to get there.

  1. 1

    Go to a park. Up goes your flag.

    Head to any park on the map. Once you’re inside the boundary, hold the button and plant your flag.

  2. 2

    A point an hour while it flies.

    Your flag earns 1 point for every hour it stays up. The longer it flies, the more it’s worth.

  3. 3

    Take parks. Win their days.

    Plant on someone else’s park and it’s yours: 4 points, plus a point for every day their flag was up. They keep what they earned.

  4. 4

    Bring your crew.

    Arrive together and hold the park as a crew. Share your crew code in the first hour and everyone’s pennant flies on the same pole.

Every hour counts.

Points never come off. Lose a park and you keep everything it earned you, then go take it back.
