Bet you didn’t know there was a park right there.

Parkdex puts every park around Victoria on your map, including the small ones tucked around the corner. Go stand inside one and its badge is yours: artwork of the view or feature that park is known for.

Android beta in Canada. No sign-up, works offline.

Every park has a badge.

Each one shows the viewpoint or landmark that park is known for, framed and starred like a collector’s card.

Browse every badge

There’s one closer than you think.

Open the map and every park nearby is a pin, from the pocket park two streets over to the waterfront patch you always drive past.

  • Tap a pin for the rarity, the drive and where to park.
  • The map lives on your phone, so a trailhead with no signal is fine.

And you can’t fake it.

Parkdex checks that you’re inside the park boundary before you can claim. Kip lets you know when you’ve arrived.

  • Snap a photo where you’re standing to claim it.
  • Every badge is dated the day you went.
  • Your location is only used for that check-in.

Rank up as you go.
