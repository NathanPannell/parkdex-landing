How it works

Three steps. No shortcuts.

You can plan from the couch, but the badge waits for you at the park. Parkdex checks your location against the real park boundary before it lets you claim.

  1. 01

    Pick a park

    Every pin is a badge. Tap one to see its rarity, the drive, and where to park.

  2. 02

    Step inside the boundary

    Head there. When you cross into the park, Kip lets you know you made it.

  3. 03

    Snap it. Claim it.

    Take a photo where you’re standing, press and hold, and the badge is yours, dated the day you went.

Built for the trail

  • Works without signal

    The map is saved on your phone, so a trailhead with no bars is fine.

  • No account needed

    Start collecting right away. A Parkdex account is optional, for backing up your badges and photos.

  • Location stays with you

    Parkdex uses your location only to check you in inside a park boundary.

Android beta

Get Parkdex in two taps.

The beta is open to anyone in Canada with an Android phone. Join the tester group, then install from Google Play like any other app.

  1. 1 Join the tester group Done

    Sign in with the Google account you use in the Play Store, then choose Join group. No approval needed.

  2. 2 Install Parkdex Opened

    Choose Become a tester, then follow the Google Play link to download Parkdex. Open it and pick your first park.

Already an internal tester? Leave the internal test in Google Play first, then do step 2. Google’s tester help
