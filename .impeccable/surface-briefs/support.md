# Public support routes

Mode: Read / Operate. Keep the support, privacy, and account-deletion routes quiet, factual, and easy to scan. Reuse the Parkdex field-guide materials: warm paper, green ink, a small lime marker, Strichpunkt headings, and Ysabeau body copy. There is no hero photograph, product mockup, decorative card stack, or italic heading on these routes.

Every route has the same small editorial header: a Parkdex home link and links to Privacy, Delete account, and Support. The content stays in one readable column with a 68ch measure. Keep the page usable at 320px, 375px, 414px, and 768px without horizontal scrolling. Maintain the shared skip link, visible focus outline, 44px navigation targets, normal reading order, and reduced-motion behavior.

Support and privacy are factual drafts with `noindex, nofollow` until the final source inventory and public copy are validated. Support identifies `contact@nathanpannell.com` as the current support and privacy channel. Reports should ask what happened, what was expected, and the device or app version; never ask for a password.

Account deletion is separate from the Android waitlist. The initial public route uses a mailto request as its primary action. The request should come from the account email where possible, describe the deletion request, and state that Nathan verifies ownership before fulfilling it and confirms completion by email, or explains unresolved cleanup. Do not invent a service level, retention promise, legal entity, or data cascade. Omit the future browser route from the customer flow until it is deployed and verified. Do not render a fake submit control or claim that opening a link deletes an account.

The waitlist explanation remains on the landing page at `/#email-privacy`. Link to it as a separate route and preserve the distinction between removing a waitlist address and deleting a Parkdex account. Use the canonical existing favicon assets and preserve the shared token names rather than introducing route-only colors or dependencies.
