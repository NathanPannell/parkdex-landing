# Parkdex landing

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated. A standalone static web application with one Vercel serverless signup endpoint. Deploy to a new Vercel project and temporary URL. No Cloudflare deployment.

## Users
People exploring Vancouver Island and nearby islands who want to discover parks and keep a record of visits.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Parkdex in one compact informational screen and register email addresses for the coming Android app.

## Positioning
Parkdex is a field guide and collection game for Vancouver Island. Its map, saved places, visit journal, and local wildlife field medals make exploration a growing personal collection.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Use real app screenshots in phone and laptop mockups. Android is coming soon, with no promised launch date. Keep the landing application independent of the current Parkdex app. Future routing is landing at parkdex.app and app at web.parkdex.app. Do not change either domain in this task. Store signup email durably and do not wake the existing app database.

## Brand Commitments
Distinct, personal, interesting, and informed by Nathan's own website at www.nathanpannell.com. Short, credible copy. Real app content and a compact single-screen composition. Existing paper, bold typography, handwritten accents, and restrained device hardware provide visual reference.

## Evidence on Hand
../parkdex/README.md establishes product facts. The live portfolio says Nathan built Parkdex to visit all 117 provincial parks on Vancouver Island. Portfolio public/media includes existing authorized app captures and material assets. No testimonials, customer metrics, or launch dates supplied.
