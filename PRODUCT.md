# Parkdex landing

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated. A standalone static web application with one Vercel serverless signup endpoint. Deploy to a new Vercel project and temporary URL. No Cloudflare deployment.

## Users
People exploring Vancouver Island and nearby islands who want to discover parks and keep a record of visits.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Parkdex with a photographic opening and four readable product stories. Register email addresses for the coming Android app, offer the current web app, and allow direct waitlist removal.

## Positioning
Parkdex is a field guide and collection game for Vancouver Island. Its map, saved places, visit journal, and local wildlife badges make exploration a growing personal collection.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Use real app screenshots in phone and laptop mockups. Android is coming soon, with no promised launch date. Keep the landing application independent of the current Parkdex app. The cutover target is landing at `parkdex.app` with the production app at `web.parkdex.app`, and landing at `staging.parkdex.app` with the staging app at `staging.web.parkdex.app`. Store signup email durably in the landing environment's own database and do not wake the existing app database.

## Brand Commitments
Distinct, personal, interesting, and informed by Nathan's own website at www.nathanpannell.com. Short, credible copy. Real app content in the user-selected Outdoor Poster composition. Oversized typography, full photography, paper story sections, a handwritten sign-off and researched device assets carry the page. No decorative eyebrows or small navigation header.

## Evidence on Hand
../parkdex/README.md establishes product facts. The live portfolio says Nathan built Parkdex to visit all 117 provincial parks on Vancouver Island. Portfolio public/media includes existing authorized app captures and material assets. No testimonials, customer metrics, or launch dates supplied.
